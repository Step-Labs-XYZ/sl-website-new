The challenge

High traffic, a story told too late.

Warmies came to us weeks before peak season with a mobile first audience and a site that wasn't selling the product fast enough.

01

The homepage lost mobile shoppers early

It drove about a third of revenue, yet the average mobile visitor saw less than a fifth of the page. The heatable, weighted and scented story sat near the bottom.

02

Order value had slipped

AOV was down roughly 24% versus the previous BFCM, and many orders landed just short of the free shipping threshold.

03

Landing pages underperformed

Themed, need based landers converted at about half the rate of collection and offer pages.

04

Peak season was close

Q4 and BFCM meant every test had to launch and learn before the code freeze.

Our approach

Evidence first, then design.

We started with data, built personas from it, and turned both into a dated roadmap the Warmies team could plan around.

  1. 01

    Diagnosis in week one

    Shopify for conversion and revenue, Clarity for scroll depth and dead clicks, plus a review of Warmies' own past tests.

  2. 02

    Persona led strategy

    Customer data surfaced six personas. We prioritised two: the Community Matriarch, the highest LTV and highest repeat segment who buys gifts for grandkids, and the Pressured Executive, focused on self care and evening routines.

  3. 03

    A roadmap with real dates

    Every project got design, feedback and launch dates, shared in a client room and a live ClickUp board.

  4. 04

    Test, don't guess

    Each redesign goes live as an Intelligems A/B test, with revenue per visitor as the primary metric rather than add to cart.

  5. 05

    Async first communication

    Daily task updates, after call action lists with owners on both sides, and weekly performance readouts in Slack.
